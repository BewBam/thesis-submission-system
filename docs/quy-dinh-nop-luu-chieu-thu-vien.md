# Quy dinh nop luu chieu luat van/luan an (Tham khao Thư viện)

Tai lieu nay tom tat noi dung thong bao ve yeu cau nop luu chieu luat van, luan an sau dai hoc. Dung lam co so cho thiet ke metadata, checklist nop bai va kiem tra dinh dang file tren he thong.

**Luu y:** Day la tai lieu tham khao theo thong bao ban cung cap. Khi trien khai chinh thuc, can doi chieu lai van ban quy dinh hien hanh cua don vi va cap nhat link bieu mau neu thay doi.

---

## 1. Noi dung

### Luan an

**Phan mot**

- Trinh bay theo bieu mau cua Phong dao tao Sau dai hoc:
  - [Huong dan trinh bay luan an tien si](https://pgs.hcmut.edu.vn/vi/tien-si/dao-tao/bieu-mau/item/2137-huong-dan-trinh-bay-luan-an-tien-si)

**Phan hai**

- Tap hop tai lieu phien hop bao ve danh gia luan an cap truong, dong vao phan cuoi cua luan an.

**Tom tat luan an**

- Trinh bay theo bieu mau:
  - [Huong dan trinh bay tom tat luan an tien si](https://pgs.hcmut.edu.vn/vi/tien-si/dao-tao/bieu-mau/item/2136-huong-dan-trinh-bay-tom-tat-luan-an-tien-si)

### Luat van

- Trinh bay theo **Bieu mau 10**:
  - [Bieu mau 10 - Mau trinh bay luat van](https://pgs.hcmut.edu.vn/vi/thac-si/hoc-vu/bieu-mau/item/75-bieu-mau-10-mau-trinh-bay-lv)

Sau bao ve:

- Noi dung phai chinh sua theo y kien nhan xet cua Hoi dong (neu co).
- Phai duoc **Can bo huong dan**, **Hoi dong bao ve**, **Ban chu nhiem khoa truc tiep** ky xac nhan thong qua noi dung.

### Cam doan danh du

- Hoc vien/Nghien cuu sinh phai **ky trong loi cam doan danh du** ve cong trinh khoa hoc cua minh.

---

## 2. Hinh thuc

### Ban in luat van/luan an

- In **mot mat** tren giay **A4**.
- Dong **bia cung ma nhung vang** (khong nhan bia in vi tinh).

### Tom tat luan an

- Dong **kim** kho **A5**, in **hai mat** ke ca bia.

### Dia CD-ROM

**Luat van**

- Toan bo noi dung luat van trong **mot file PDF**.
- Kem cac file chuong trinh, file tinh toan... neu co yeu cau tu Khoa, Can bo huong dan.

**Luan an**

- **Ghep ca hai phan** (scan phan hai) thanh **mot file PDF**.
- Kem **tom tat luan an tien si**.

**So luong, chat luong, mau nhan dia, hop kep chua CD-ROM...**

- Quy dinh chi tiet tai **Bieu mau 12**:
  - [Bieu mau 12 - Quy dinh nop luat van va mau trinh bay nhan dia CD luu luat van](https://pgs.hcmut.edu.vn/vi/thac-si/hoc-vu/bieu-mau/item/77-bieu-mau-12-quy-dinh-nop-lv-va-mau-trinh-bay-nhan-dia-cd-luu-lv)

## 3. Ho tro kiem tra dinh dang

Thong bao ghi nhan: Thu vien co **tu van, ho tro kiem tra dinh dang** luat van/luan an theo quy dinh truoc khi in an va ghi CD-ROM.

**Lien he:** Bo phan Dich vu Tham khao - dien thoai **3864 7256 (5419)**.

---

## 4. Gợi ý map sang he thong web (giai doan hien tai: FE/BE, chua DSpace)

Cac muc co the chuyen thanh yeu cau phan mem sau:

- Checkbox/xac nhan: da chinh sua theo Hoi dong, da co ky xac nhan cua CBHD/Hoi dong/Ban chu nhiem khoa (upload scan hoac buoc workflow).
- Upload bat buoc: **PDF toan van** (luat van: 1 file; luan an: 1 file ghep 2 phan + file tom tat rieng neu quy dinh tach).
- Upload them: phu luc (chuong trinh, tinh toan...) neu co.
- Metadata: loai tai lieu (luat van/luan an), link bieu mau tham chieu, ghi chu ho tro thu vien.
